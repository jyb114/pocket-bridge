Dot on your phone

Reach your existing dot through the official ChatGPT mobile app when its supporting update is available.

Use the official app

Dot is a cloud agent. OpenAI currently supports it in the desktop app and desktop web, with mobile app access rolling out. Mobile web is not supported.

Pocket Bridge currently has no Dot messaging connection. This guide does not load your Dot conversations, activity, or approval requests.

Continue with your dot

  1. Create your dot on desktop first. Use the ChatGPT desktop app or a desktop browser.
  2. Open the ChatGPT mobile app. Sign in to the same account and choose your dot. If it is missing, check for the supporting app update and account rollout.
  3. Use its own conversation and request controls. Send instructions there. Review activity and approval requests in official ChatGPT wherever those controls are available.

Let your dot use this computer

In the desktop ChatGPT app, open your dot’s profile. Under Computers, find Your computer and select Allow access. Review and accept the app’s confirmation.

Keep the computer online with the ChatGPT app open for local work. Your dot’s cloud work can continue while the computer is off.
